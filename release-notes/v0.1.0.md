First test build.

**Status: early.** Not yet tested on a real phone. Set it up with protection off first, and only turn protection on once blocking works the way you expect.

## Install
Download `Flip-0.1.0.apk` below on your phone and open it. Then open Flip, tap the right soft key (**FLIP**) and work through Setup. Full steps are in the [README](https://github.com/PresWiFi/flip#setup-on-the-phone).

## What's in it
- **Home screen:** Razr V3, V3 with an outer screen, or Modern. Keypad with dial + T9 search, dial only, or none. Main menu of 9 apps you choose.
- **Blocking:** always blocked, daily limits, blocked hours and group limits, optionally hiding notifications. Works however the app is opened.
- **Unlocking:** 3 quick unlocks a day (5 min), scan a tag, type a 24-character code, or the emergency code (15 min each).
- **Protection:** uninstall lock, settings guard, a code to loosen any rule, and a log of every unlock and attempt.

Future releases are signed with the same key, so they update this one in place and keep your settings. Android may warn about an unknown developer. Flip never uploads your data.
