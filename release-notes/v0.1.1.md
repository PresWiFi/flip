The Razr V3 look now looks like a V3.

## Install
Download `Flip-0.1.1.apk` below on your phone and open it. It updates 0.1.0 in place and keeps your settings.

## New
- **V3 keypad:** one flat etched sheet with blue backlit symbols instead of separate buttons, laid out like the V3: soft keys and the menu key on top, search and C either side of a round 5-way navigation key, then send, end and the digits.
- **5-way key:** moves the highlight through the main menu and search results; the center opens the highlighted app. On the idle screen the center opens the main menu.
- **V3 body:** earpiece slot above the screen, barrel hinge, brushed-metal finish, curved chin with the mic hole.
- **Colors:** silver, black or pink (Flip settings → Look). The outer screen matches.
- The main menu title shows the highlighted app's name, like the V3.
